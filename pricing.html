Subitem Reports — Pricing

Last updated: 2026-10-16

One flat price per account, every user included. Billed by monday.com. The only thing that changes between plans is how much you take out of the widget: how many boards one report combines, which exports you get, how far back the trend goes, and whether the rollup shows what changed.

Every install starts a 14-day Business trial, no card needed. When the trial ends the account moves to Free and nothing is deleted: saved reports keep rendering (a multi-board report shows its first board), the trend keeps its last 30 days live, and exports, extra boards and the older history wait for a plan.

Free — $0

Subitem charts, rollups, workload and a 30-day trend for one board. No card, no time limit.

  • Chart, Rollup table, Workload and Trend views for one board per report
  • Group by any column; count, sum, average, min, max or % done using your board's own done labels
  • Status filter, goal line, items / subitems / both, multi-level subitems
  • Trend: the last 30 days, recorded whenever your dashboard is opened
  • Every user in your account, unlimited widgets, light/dark/black themes

Team — $29/month, or $276/year ($23/month) · Recommended

For one team's dashboard: combine up to 5 boards, export CSV and PNG, keep 13 months of trend history.

  • Everything in Free
  • Up to 5 boards per report, a whole Standard-plan dashboard
  • Export every view: CSV, PNG and copy as image
  • Trend: 13 months of history, change badges vs 7 and 30 days ago, trend CSV
  • One flat price per account, every user included; billed by monday.com

Business — $79/month, or $756/year ($63/month) · Trial plan

For PMOs and agencies reporting to others: unlimited boards, PDF reports and what-changed deltas.

  • Everything in Team
  • Unlimited boards per report: portfolio rollups across every board on your dashboard
  • PDF report with title, logo and "prepared for" line, ready to send to a client
  • What changed: moved to Done, newly stuck and per-parent deltas since your last visit, in the table and the PDF

Limits

Boards per report is the only limit: 1 on Free, 5 on Team, unlimited on Business (within monday's own dashboard limits). Nothing else is metered: not users, not widgets, not exports, not actions.

Billing

  • Plans are bought and billed by monday.com through its app billing. The plan picker opens from the widget; only an account admin can buy.
  • Prices are in US dollars, monthly or yearly. Yearly is about 20% less than twelve monthly payments.
  • Cancel within 30 days of any charge (a first purchase or a renewal) for a full refund of that charge, handled through monday.com billing.
  • If you cancel or downgrade, the account moves to Free when monday ends the paid plan. Nothing is deleted.

AI

"Describe a report" is optional and included on every plan; it uses your account's monday AI credits and needs the account's AI gateway permission. It only writes report settings: every number in the widget is computed from your boards, never by the model.

Support

E-mail support on every plan, with a reply within 2 business days. What support covers is on the support page.

On every plan

Read-only board access. Runs entirely inside monday with no external servers. Report settings and trend history live in your monday account's app storage, shared with the people in your account who use the widget. Nothing is e-mailed and nothing is stored outside monday. You can delete recorded history from the widget at any time; the privacy policy has the details.
