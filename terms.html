1. What the app is

Subitem Reports is a dashboard widget for monday.com. It charts, rolls up and exports subitems and items from the boards you select, shows workload by person and week, and records a trend of a report's metric over time. The widget is read-only: it cannot create, change, move or delete anything in your boards. It runs in your browser inside monday.com, and it saves its settings and history only inside your monday account. I operate no server that the widget communicates with. The features of each plan are listed on the pricing page and in monday's plan picker.
