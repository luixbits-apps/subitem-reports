Subitem Reports support

Last updated: 2026-10-16

How to reach me

E-mail contact@luizperren.dev, the only support channel, answered within 2 business days (Monday to Friday, Swiss public holidays excluded). Put "Urgent" in the subject if the widget fails for your whole account or you suspect a security problem.

What support covers

  • Questions about using the app, and bug reports.
  • Deleting the app's data for an account: write from an account admin's address and include the monday account id.

It does not cover changing your boards or dashboards, or custom work. Plans, payments and refunds are handled by monday.com.

Self-help

The guide explains every view, export and error message. See also Pricing, the Privacy Policy and the Terms of Service.
