1. How to reach me and how fast I reply

  • E-mail contact@luizperren.dev. This is the only support channel; there is no phone or chat support. In the widget, the Help menu entry "Contact support" opens a new e-mail to this address.
  • First reply within 2 business days of your message. Accounts on the Business plan get a first reply within 1 business day. Business days are Monday to Friday, excluding public holidays at my place of business in Switzerland. The mailbox is read every business day.
  • Urgent issues (the widget fails for your whole account, or you suspect a security problem): put "Urgent" or "Security" at the start of the subject line. These are handled first.
  • Reply times apply to a first substantive reply. A fix for a bug ships as a new app version, which monday installs on your account automatically; I tell you when it is live, but cannot promise a date in advance.
  • Replies are in English.

2. What support covers

  • Installing the app and adding the widget to a dashboard.
  • Configuring a report: boards, rows, levels, grouping, aggregation, done labels, at-risk labels, status filter, goal line, view.
  • Understanding the figures: how "% done", rollups, the "What changed" column, workload hours and trend recording work, and why a figure differs from what you expected.
  • Exports: CSV, PNG, copy as image and the PDF report (title, logo, "prepared for" line).
  • Plans and the trial: what each plan includes, how to upgrade or downgrade, what pauses when the trial ends.
  • Errors, slow loading, display problems in the light, dark and black themes, keyboard and screen-reader use.
  • Privacy questions and data deletion requests (section 6).
  • The optional "Describe a report" AI action: what it sends, and the error messages it shows.

3. What support cannot do

  • I cannot see your boards or dashboards. The widget runs inside your monday account and stores nothing with me, so I rely on your description and screenshots.
  • I never change your dashboard, widget settings or boards. I tell you what to click; you make the change. The one exception is deleting everything the app stored for your account on a verified admin request (section 6).
  • I cannot enable monday AI for your account, add AI credits, or grant the AI gateway permission; your monday admin does that in monday.
  • I cannot change monday's own limits (boards per dashboard, items per dashboard, API rate limits) or fix problems in monday.com itself. For those, contact monday.com support.
  • I cannot issue refunds or invoices myself. monday.com bills the app and processes refunds; I can ask monday on your behalf (section 5).
  • I do not provide custom development, consulting on your board structure, or support for other apps.
  • I do not help with circumventing plan limits.

4. What to include in your message

  • Your monday account id or account URL (yourcompany.monday.com).
  • The dashboard and widget concerned, your plan, and whether you were in the dashboard's edit mode.
  • What you did, what you expected, and what happened, with the exact text of any error message (the "Technical details" line under an error helps).
  • A screenshot, with names and confidential content blurred or cropped.
  • Your browser and whether the dashboard uses the light, dark or black theme.
  • Never send passwords, monday session tokens, API tokens, or exports that contain personal data unless I ask for them and you are allowed to share them.

5. Plans, trial, billing and refunds

  • Every installation starts a 14-day Business trial. When it ends the account moves to Free and nothing is deleted; paid features resume when you subscribe. The pricing page lists what each plan includes.
  • To buy, change or cancel a plan, an account admin uses monday's plan picker (the plan badge in the widget's Edit report bar, for example "Free plan · see plans", or the upgrade buttons) or monday's billing settings. monday.com bills you and issues the invoices.
  • Refunds: you may cancel within 30 days of any charge and receive a full refund of that charge. Ask monday.com's billing support, or e-mail me and I will ask monday to process it. See the Terms of Service, section 6.
  • Trial extensions: e-mail me before your trial ends if you need more time.

6. Requesting data deletion

You can delete the app's stored history yourself, at any time, without contacting me:

  • One report's trend history and What changed record: in the dashboard's edit mode, open the Trend view and choose "Delete this history".
  • All of the app's history for your account (every trend series, What changed record and usage counter): an account admin, in the dashboard's edit mode, chooses Help menu → "Delete all Subitem Reports history".

To have me delete everything instead, report settings included, an admin of the monday account e-mails contact@luizperren.dev with the account id and the subject "Delete Subitem Reports history". I verify that the request comes from an admin of that account, then run monday's developer command mapps storage:remove-data -a 12150920 -c <accountId>, which removes all of the app's storage for the account at once (every widget's saved report settings, every trend series, What changed record and usage counter), and confirm by e-mail within 10 days. Removing a widget stops its report settings from being read but does not delete them. Uninstalling the app removes the widget from your dashboards; I do not claim that it purges app storage by itself, so delete the history first, or ask me to delete everything. Details are in the Privacy Policy.

7. Status and incidents

  • There is no separate status page. If an incident affects many accounts I post a dated notice at the top of this page and keep it updated until the incident is resolved.
  • The widget runs on monday.com's platform. When everything in monday is slow or failing, check monday's status page first (https://status.monday.com/).
  • If I become aware of a security incident involving the app I notify monday.com within 24 hours and affected accounts without undue delay.

8. Feature requests and reviews

  • Send feature requests to contact@luizperren.dev with the subject "Subitem Reports feature request"; the Help menu entry "Request a feature" starts that e-mail for you. I read every request and decide what to build by how many accounts ask for it; I cannot promise a feature or a date.
  • If support solved your problem, a review on the marketplace listing helps other teams find the app: Help menu → "Rate Subitem Reports", or open yourcompany.monday.com/apps/installed_apps/12150920?openRatingDialog=true with your own account's address.
