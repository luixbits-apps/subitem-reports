Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-16

Provider: Luiz Anton Perren (LuixBits), Oberschönenbuch 31, 6438 Ibach, Switzerland. Contact: contact@luizperren.dev.

The short version

Subitem Reports runs entirely inside monday.com. It has no servers of its own, sends nothing to me, and uses no analytics, cookies or advertising. Nothing is sold. What the app saves stays in your monday.com account.

What the app reads

The board data the viewing user may see, read through monday's API in that user's browser to draw the report. It is not sent to me and is not stored anywhere else.

What the app stores

In your account's monday.com app storage:

  • each widget's report settings, including a PDF title, "prepared for" line and logo you enter;
  • trend history: one number per day per report metric (up to 400 days), with a short description built from column titles and status labels;
  • "What changed" records: per parent item its item id, value, status counts and two yes/no flags (all subitems done, any at risk);
  • usage counters (most boards combined, exports, recorded days, first use).

History holds no item names, people names or other cell text. Your browser keeps a few viewing preferences, such as the last view and that you accepted the terms notice.

AI, only when you use "Describe a report"

Your request, the board names, the column titles and types, and the item and subitem counts go to monday.com's AI gateway on your account's AI credits. monday passes them to its AI providers under monday's AI terms (https://monday.com/l/legal/ai/). No cell values, item names or people.

Who else is involved

  • monday.com Ltd. (Israel) hosts the app, its storage and all billing; its own privacy policy applies.
  • Hostpoint AG (Switzerland) hosts my support mailbox.
  • GitHub, Inc. (USA) hosts these pages and keeps standard server logs (IP address, page, time).

Israel has an adequacy decision of Switzerland and the EU; transfers to the USA rely on the Swiss-US and EU-US Data Privacy Framework.

Roles

Your organization controls the data in its monday.com account. I see none of it in normal operation; I can reach the app's storage for your account only through monday's developer tools, and use them only when your account's admin asks me to. I am responsible for support e-mails and these pages (Swiss FADP; GDPR where it applies, on the bases of contract and legitimate interest, Art. 6(1)(b) and (f)).

Retention and deletion

  • History: "Delete this history" in the Trend view removes one report's history; "Delete all Subitem Reports history" in the Help menu (account admins) removes all of it. Points older than 400 days drop off.
  • Report settings: change them in the widget. Removing the widget or the app stops them being read but does not delete them.
  • Everything at once: an admin can e-mail me the account id and I delete all of the app's storage for that account within 10 days.
  • Support e-mails: deleted 12 months after a case closes; Hostpoint's backups expire within 180 days.

Your rights

For access, correction or deletion, e-mail contact@luizperren.dev. You may complain to the Swiss FDPIC or to the data protection authority where you live.
